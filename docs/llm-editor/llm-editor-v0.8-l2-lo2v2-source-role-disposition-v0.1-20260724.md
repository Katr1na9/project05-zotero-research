# LO2v2 source-role disposition

## Verdict

**Reject the train/development replacement and downgrade LO2v2 to inactive metadata-only negative source-gate evidence.**

Portfolio direction 04 remains vacant. No family, lineage, sample, or quota credit is awarded.

## Evidence

The committed bounded audit established useful structural facts:

- source and reader identity passed;
- the index parsed as JSON;
- 115 distinct run candidates matched the curator-declared count;
- all 115 candidates exposed a detected 54-item test-container shape.

Those facts do not establish an evidence-safe model-visible source. The same frozen audit found:

- 6,785 protected label or supervision token matches;
- 6,325 test or task identity candidates;
- 219,123 privacy or identifier token matches;
- no nested-notice closure;
- no v1/v2 overlap comparison;
- no global completion, duplicate, retry, partial-run, repeated-system, or statistical-independence proof;
- no bound, source-recoverable pointer.

The terminal status is `fail_closed_protected_label_surface_detected`.

## Why the positive structure does not rescue the source

Valid JSON, 115 candidate runs, a consistent 54-item container, and deterministic digest construction are capacity evidence only. They cannot override protected-label, privacy, notice, overlap, lineage, independence, or pointer Gates.

The 54-item surface is particularly unsuitable as sample capacity because the audit detected protected test/task identity. The 115 run candidates are not globally verified independent completed executions.

## Forbidden remediation

Do not:

- narrow, remove, rename, whitelist, downweight, or reinterpret label/privacy tokens;
- rerun with changed rules, thresholds, caps, projections, parsers, or authorities;
- select only fields, subtrees, rows, runs, tests, tasks, statuses, or services that avoid the frozen probes;
- count tests, tasks, services, metrics, traces, files, rows, timestamps, or 54-item containers as independent executions;
- treat in-memory pointer/digest construction as pointer binding;
- switch automatically to broader logs, metrics, traces, raw runtime archives, another revision, v1, or a transformed table.

Any future reconsideration requires a new immutable curator-published, physically separate, label-free and privacy-safe manifest surface. It must enter a new metadata review and new acquisition/audit authority; it is not a rerun of this rejected index.

## Scope

No token rule changed, no effective catalog was written, no role or credit changed, no training sample was generated, and no baseline, fine-tuning, Kernel, Gamma, M3, or L2 action occurred.
